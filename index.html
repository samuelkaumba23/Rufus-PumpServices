Our Services

Complete pump solutions for industrial, commercial and agricultural clients. We work with all major brands.

Pump Installation

Professional installation of centrifugal, submersible, positive displacement and specialized pumps. Correct sizing, alignment and commissioning.

  • Site assessment & design
  • Piping & electrical works
  • Testing & handover

Pump Repairs

Diagnosis and repair of mechanical seals, bearings, impellers, shafts, motors and control systems. Workshop and on-site service.

  • Fault diagnosis
  • Seal & bearing replacement
  • Performance restoration

Component Replacement

Genuine and high-quality aftermarket parts. Impellers, mechanical seals, shafts, wear rings, motors, and control boards.

  • OEM & compatible parts
  • Fast sourcing
  • Proper fitting & testing

Automation Configurations

VFD setup, PLC integration, level control, pressure systems, SCADA connectivity and smart pump monitoring.

  • VFD programming
  • Sensor & control panels
  • Remote monitoring setup

Spares & Accessories

Stock of common spares plus special order. Couplings, base plates, valves, strainers, gauges, cables and more.

  • Common spares in stock
  • Custom orders
  • Delivery available

Emergency Call-Outs

Priority response for critical pump failures. Available for industrial and commercial clients on service contracts.

  • 24/7 options available
  • Contract priority
  • Rapid diagnosis
